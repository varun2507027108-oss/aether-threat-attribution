Custody Chain Verification

Load an exported AETHER custody ledger (aether_custody_ledger_*.csv) to independently recompute every SHA-256 link in the chain of custody. This page runs entirely in your browser: no file, no case identifier, and no key material is transmitted anywhere. Verification is exactly the procedure described under Section 63, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (formerly s.65B, Indian Evidence Act, 1872) — the output here is a technical integrity finding, not a certificate of authenticity.

2 · Optional signature checkchecking

Chain integrity alone detects edits. A signature check additionally defeats the recompute attack, where an attacker edits one entry and re-hashes every entry after it — the hash chain still verifies, but the signature does not.

Scope of this finding. A passing result means the file you hold is internally consistent: each entry's stored hash equals the SHA-256 of its own contents, and each entry references the hash of the one before it. It does not establish who created the file, when, or that the observations recorded within it are true. A failing result means the file was altered after it was produced. Internal consistency is necessary, never sufficient, for Section 63, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (formerly s.65B, Indian Evidence Act, 1872) certification; the certificate and its examiner declaration must be obtained from the investigating officer.
